# 🌍 AirGuard — AI-Powered Air Quality Intelligence

> **From measuring pollution to predicting it — and knowing what to do next.**

AirGuard is an AI-powered air quality intelligence platform designed to transform real-time environmental data into **actionable, location-aware health and pollution insights**.

Instead of simply displaying an AQI number, AirGuard answers three critical questions:

**What is the air quality now? → What is likely to happen next? → What should people do about it?**

## 🚀 What Makes AirGuard Different?

- 📡 **Real-Time Air Quality** — Integrates live monitoring data from OpenAQ.
- 🌦️ **Weather Intelligence** — Uses Open-Meteo weather and air-quality data.
- 🧮 **CPCB-Based AQI** — Converts pollutant measurements into meaningful AQI information.
- 🔮 **Predictive Analytics** — Forecasts pollution trends and identifies potential future hotspots.
- 🤖 **Gemini AI Intelligence** — Explains *why* pollution may change and converts predictions into actionable recommendations.
- 🎓 **Student Smart Mode** — Personalized guidance for commuting, outdoor classes and sports.
- 🫁 **Respiratory Sensitivity Mode** — General exposure-reduction guidance for people with asthma or airway sensitivity.
- 🚨 **Smart Alerts** — Detects pollution spikes, rising trends and potential exposure risks.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Pollution Map** — Visualizes monitoring stations and pollution hotspots.
- 📊 **Explainable Predictions** — Shows the factors influencing forecasts instead of presenting a black-box prediction.
